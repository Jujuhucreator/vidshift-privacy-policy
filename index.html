Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how VidShift ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary for the app to function correctly and for us to provide support:

  • Account Information: When you sign in via Google or Apple, we receive your email address and basic profile information to identify your account.
  • Device Identifiers: We collect push notification tokens and device IDs to deliver service-related notifications and for diagnostic purposes.
  • User Content: If you use the "Report Bug" feature, we collect the text description and any screenshots you voluntarily upload.
  • Usage Data: We may collect diagnostic logs if you encounter an error, to help us fix technical issues.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our service (authentication and database storage).
  • To send you important notifications and updates.
  • To respond to your bug reports and provide customer support.
  • To improve the performance and stability of the application.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services which may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google & Apple Sign-In: For secure authentication.
  • Firebase (Google): For data storage (Firestore), file storage, and authentication.
  • Expo: For delivering push notifications and app updates.

4. Data Retention and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase's industry-standard encryption. We retain your information as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. You can do this within the app settings or by contacting us directly.
